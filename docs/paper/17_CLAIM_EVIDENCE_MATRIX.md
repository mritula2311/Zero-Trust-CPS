## Claim version and causal status

Canonical baseline artifacts are associated with main correction `4f6afa2` and the preserved exact replay; additional nonfinite protection begins at branch commit `6d74a0e` and is extended by this audit. Historical 10/20-node results have separate commit/blob identities. M1–M9 and seed-study JSONs do not all carry producing commits or checkpoint fingerprints, so code/artifact compatibility is audited but immutable original execution attestation is incomplete.

C01/C02/C06 are implementation-contract claims supported by tests; C04/C05/C07/C11/C12 are descriptive/associational comparisons within specified corpora. C08 supports a controlled intervention interpretation conditional on the constructed probes, not a generalized field causal claim. C10/C14 are limited provenance/physical-evidence statements. No claim in this matrix establishes literature priority without verified related work.

The Abstract may describe the dual-score architecture and implemented explanation/audit capability. Configured M6 serving is a software fact, not a verified comparative or physical deployment result. Quantitative superiority, trained-policy lineage, hardware-scale and production-security claims require the corresponding evidence gates. No wording may remove these qualifiers.
